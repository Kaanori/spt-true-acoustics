# Changelog

## 0.8.8-beta

**Version numbers:** this release is **0.8.8** everywhere (Forge, GitHub, DLL/log). The two hotfixes before it were published as 0.8.6 and 0.8.7 on the Forge and as v0.8.5.1 and v0.8.5.2 on GitHub – same files, different numbers. From now on there is one number.

The big update after 0.8.5: real decibels with one loudness curve, doors by their real leaf, sound from the direction of the opening it really comes through, several ways at once, the close recording of gunshots at every distance, reverb from measured rooms, and a set of fixes for bugs in the game's own audio system. Contains everything of 0.8.5.1 and 0.8.5.2.

Labels: **[heard]** played in raids and judged by ear by the tester · **[log]** running in raids, the logs (or a recording) show it works, not separately judged by ear · **[built]** built and offline-tested, running in the tester's game, not yet verified in a raid · **[off]** experimental, off by default.

**Defaults are the author's tested settings.** **Updating:** BepInEx keeps your saved values. To get the tested state, delete `BepInEx/config/kaan.materialklang.cfg` once (your own changes are lost) or set the switches listed under *Changed → Defaults* by hand.

### Added

- **[heard] Loudness curve** (`0. Physics Mode → Compression knee (dB(A))` 85, `Compression above knee` 0.2). Insulation counts in real decibels; above a real level of 85 dB(A) at your ear each real dB counts 0.2 dB, like the game's own mix squeezes loud sounds (its close gunshot clips play like 93–96 dB(A) for a real ~140 dB(A)). Soft knee, 10 dB wide. Tested by ear against a knee of 95 (shots behind gates too quiet) and set back to 85.
- **[log] Explosions: compression from the game distance curve** (on): explosions behind walls are squeezed with the slope of the game's own explosion curve (~0.31) instead of the gunshot slope; a grenade 43 m away behind 3–4 rooms plays 7.2 dB instead of 4.6 dB quieter.
- **[heard] on Factory: `13. Distance (SEB) → Near recording at every distance (physics does the rest)`** (on in this release). Other people's shots always play the close recording; distance comes from physics only (level, air over the whole distance, walls). `Near recording: width shrinks with distance` (on) narrows the stereo width with distance (`Near recording: source width (m)` 2, an estimate).
- **[log] `Near recording: distance through the loudness curve`** (on): beyond 5 m the drop over distance goes through the same loudness curve as walls, from 140 dB(A) at 1 m (suppressed 120 dB(A)). Rifle re 5 m: 55 m −4 dB, 400 m −8 dB (plain 1/r −21 / −38 dB). Added because plain 1/r made distant shots far too quiet. In a Customs raid it applied to every shot of other players and bots (up to 400 m); not yet judged by ear on the large maps.
- **[built] Calibrated source levels** (`17. All fixed sources → Calibrate source levels`, on): sources without game occlusion (V-Ex taxi, BTR, trains, plane, fires, generators, machines, dripping water, alarms) play at their real level relative to footsteps (reference measured from other players' steps in the raid; corrections −12 … +3 dB). Their distance curve keeps the game curve only up to its flat start and then follows the source's own law (point source 1/r; long sources like BTR, train, burning car flatter near by).
- **[built] Lamps at a real hum level** (`Lamps: real hum level (direct sound + room field)` on, `Lamps: hum level at 1 m (dB(A))` 45): direct sound 1/r plus the reverberant field of the lamp's room, then the room paths as for every source. The level 45 dB(A) is an estimate for worn ballasts; the BepInEx log says per lamp how much quieter it now plays (`[Calibration] Lamp …`).
- **[log] Direction from the opening** (`Room paths: direction from the opening`, on): sound through doors and windows is heard from the opening it comes out of. With openings on different sides the **first sound front** leads (`Room paths: direction from the leading path`, `Room paths: first sound front from all paths`); one-shot sounds (impacts, casings, doors, windows, grenades) follow it too (`Room paths: direction for one-shot sounds`); the diffuse part comes from the openings as well (`Room paths: diffuse part from the openings`).
- **[log] Several ways as separate voices** (`Room paths: second path as its own voice`, on; `Room paths: voices per sound` 3; `Room paths: voice pool size` 16): each clearly different way plays as its own copy from its opening, with its own share of energy, timbre and travel delay; all copies together are as loud as one sound before. For enemy footsteps/gear, voices and one-shot sounds (sub-switches), never for your own sounds or gunshots.
- **[log] Invisible carrier for lamps** (`Room paths: carrier for fixed sources with visible parts`, on): lamp hum can be moved to the opening without moving the lamp model.
- **Precomputed obstacle paths – code only** (`19. … → Precomputed obstacle paths`, on; `Precomputed paths: line-of-sight check` on; `Obstacle paths: direction from the edge` on): inside buildings, when the game finds no way around an obstacle, level and direction can come from a table built in advance per map. **The map data is not part of this release** (the tables built in testing are bound to one installation); without it the ISO 9613-2 screening limit applies (at most 20 dB for one edge, 25 dB for several) and the mod runs normally. The log line about a missing table is an info line, not a warning.
- **[log] `14. Sound Paths → Low-pass fit for the 24 dB/oct game filter`** (on in this release): the game's low-pass per sound falls 24 dB per octave (measured), not 12 as assumed; the cut-off is now fitted for the real filter – shots through doors keep their highs.
- **[log] `0. Physics Mode → No bass cut on the path (high-pass)`** (on in this release): the game's 80 Hz high-pass cut 15–27 dB at 63–100 Hz off every shot; now 10 Hz on paths the mod calculates. A recording showed the bass back within about 3 dB of the clip.
- **[log] `7. Weapon Directivity → Occluded shots: no extra occluded mix (physics)`** (on in this release): Sound Engine Backport played an extra unfiltered copy of occluded shots (up to +6 dB, jump at its threshold); with Physics Mode on these shots stay in the normal gunshot mix.
- **[built] `Timbre follows loudness curve`** (on): loud sounds that the curve keeps loud behind an obstacle also lose only as much treble as their loudness drop says.
- **[log] Diffuse part of sounds from other rooms:** `Diffuse sound as reverb` (on) splits room paths into a directed and a diffuse part; `Diffuse sound stays audible` (on) plays the diffuse part with the dry sound, because the game's reverb plays it 30–50 dB too quietly (measured on Factory); `Diffuse sound: only what arrives with the event` (on, short sounds: 125 ms window) and `Speech from other rooms: only the intelligible early part` (on, D50) limit it. `Obstacle in the same room keeps the reverb` (on).
- **[log] Reverb zones per part of a room** (`9. Reverb by Material → Max zone boxes` 2000) instead of one box over the whole room; rooms are the union of their boxes, reverb time by Eyring, open portals with their real area. `Reverb level follows reverb time` (on).
- **[log] `Room absorption from random chords`** (on): absorption of each room from random lines through its volume, so furniture, machines and walkways count. Big halls on Factory: 6–7 s → about 4–5 s at 1 kHz. Measured in the background at the first raid on a map (1 ms per frame) and cached.
- **[built] Doors: gate wings** (`4. Doors and Windows → Gate wings: count with the opening`, on); **door leaf only on the room path** (`5. Walls Between Rooms → Door/window leaf: room path only`, on) – no double counting.
- **[built] Outdoors without a game route:** `Room paths: diffraction when the game has no route` (on, ISO 9613-2 screening over roofs and terrain) and `Room paths: housing along the path (ISO 9613-2 A.3)` (on, up to 10 dB for rows of houses).
- **[built] Wall damping of non-occluded sources:** `Wall damping: air absorption (ISO 9613-1)` (on), `Wall damping: ground under the path is no wall` (on), `Wall damping: real audibility` (on, masking by an outdoor background of 40 dB(A)).
- **[built] `7. Weapon Directivity → Cover SEB legacy (V1) weapons`** (on): weapons that SEB plays with its older engine get directivity, the distance rules and the suppressor flag too.
- **[log] Test source** (section `20. Test source (measurement)`): place a sound where you stand and play it from elsewhere (click, pink noise, a game clip or a real gunshot; repeat; reference tick).
- **[off]** `21. Gunshot spatialization (experimental) → Gunshots: HRTF (experimental)`; `22. Meta occlusion (experimental) → Override Meta occlusion intensity` (for measurements only); `19. Path search (experimental)` (needs a path network per map that is not shipped); `11. … → Save heard sounds for the demo` (WAV files of heard clips for the demo page, stay on your PC).
- Sound log: many new fields (direction, voices, diffuse part, lamp level, close-recording level), `ids src#… occ#… calc#…` to tell sources of the same name apart, logs outside a raid named `…-outside-raid.log`.

### Changed

- **Defaults = the author's tested settings.** Changed defaults (set them by hand if you keep your old cfg):
  - `0. Physics Mode (real values)`: `Loudness factor` 0.2 → **1** (real physics; loud sounds are handled by the loudness curve); `No bass cut on the path (high-pass)` **on**; `Quiet sources: real audibility` **off**; `Room paths: diffuse part as real reverb` **off**
  - `7. Weapon Directivity`: `Occluded shots: no extra occluded mix (physics)` **on**
  - `9. Reverb by Material`: `Min. absorption` **0.08** (no effect any more)
  - `10. Advanced (rays)`: `Transmission rays x` 1 → **4**, `Reflection rays x` 1 → **4**, `Max. bounces` 0 → **6** (from the next raid; more rays cost more CPU)
  - `13. Distance (SEB)`: `Enabled` off → **on**; `Near recording at every distance (physics does the rest)` **on**
  - `14. Sound Paths (physics)`: `Band low-pass strength` 1.0 → **0.6**; `Low-pass fit for the 24 dB/oct game filter` **on**
  - `15. Ambient emitters`: `Indoor room tone volume (%)` 100 → **30**
  - `3. Bot Hearing (SAIN) → Mode` stays **Off**.
- **Doors and windows insulate by their own leaf** (section 4): game material and measured thickness of each leaf/pane, mass law and coincidence per band, plus an unsealed gap (doors 0.5 %, windows 0.1 % of the area – at most about 23 / 30 dB). Metal doors thicker than a sheet count as two 1.5 mm sheets. New settings `Door leaf: use measured thickness`, `Fallback thickness: wood/plastic leaf (m)` (0.04), `Fallback thickness: glass pane (m)` (0.006), `Metal door: sheet thickness (mm)` (1.5).
- **Walls:** several walls in a row count wall by wall with the rooms in between; glass panes and double shells count as such; the reverberant field of the source room drives the wall (EN ISO 12354-1); thin metal sheet 2 mm (was 3 mm); thick steel plates harder.
- **Room paths:** the sound field of the source room goes through the first opening (was only the direct part); a big opening radiates towards where you stand (bunker hatch: much quieter beside and above it) and its edges diffract; edge diffraction uses the real angle of incidence (on average 2–3 dB quieter than before); routes blend per frame.
- **Section 13:** `Enabled` is on by default now. With the close recording on, its distance curve and `Far clip compensation (dB)` are not used for other people's shots; it then extends the outdoor range of unsuppressed shots to 900 m and keeps the flatter explosion curve.
- `14. Sound Paths → Receiving room: diffuse share` and `Receiving room: max. correction (dB)` have no effect any more (the wall path now computes both rooms).

### Fixed

- **[heard] Closed doors muted gunfire completely in local raids** and **low-pass flutter ("robot sound")** – from 0.8.5.1.
- **[log] SEB 1.1.x** accepted – from 0.8.7 (on GitHub v0.8.5.2).
- **[log] One-shot sounds start only after their occlusion** (`One-shot sounds start only after their occlusion`, on): impacts and ricochets behind walls could slip out at full volume for one audio block (about 1/50 s) – a recording showed 30 such spikes of +17 … +52 dB.
- **[log] Doors, containers, switches and breaking windows** start silent too (same switch; they register their sound only after starting it). In a Customs raid all door and window sounds in other rooms started muffled.
- **[log] Grenade bounces behind walls were never occluded** (pooled source kept the previous owner's shorter range): `Occlusion range follows the playing sound` (on).
- **[built] The game's audio culling only ever handled its last 10 looping sources** – fires, the V-Ex taxi, machines and lamps could stay off or silent for a whole raid; loops stopped by the audio reset at raid start are restarted (`1. General → Fix game audio culling (all looping sources)`, on; works without Physics Mode).
- **[built] Footsteps flared up at their start** by 15–25 dB (a source between two sounds got a level computed without a sound): `Idle sources keep their level` (on).
- **[log] Clicks and "robot sound"** from the dry part of room paths and from the reverb send – no clicks of the mod found in later raid recordings.
- **[built]** The V-Ex taxi, BTR and generators were too loud or cut off hard at distance, or came straight through buildings (Streets, Ground Zero); a flashbang 24 m away in the open was silent; edge diffraction around a 90° corner was up to 24 dB too loud at large angles; the main switch `1. General → Enabled = off` now really gives vanilla everywhere; one failing hook no longer stops the following ones; the sound log keeps MARK lines when it reaches its size limit.

### Removed or switched off

- **Switched off after a listening test: `Room paths: diffuse part as real reverb`** (a real reverb tail for sounds from other rooms, with its sub-switches). It ran in raids, but every variant sounded wrong to the tester; only the dry version sounded right. Off by default, not developed further.
- **`Quiet sources: real audibility`** (masking of quiet sounds by an assumed background) is **off** by default now – it changed what you hear by a rule about hearing, not by physics.
- Removed: the fixed door values `Door thin wood`, `Door thick wood`, `Door thin metal`, `Door thick metal`, `Window intact` (replaced by the leaf model).
- No longer used: `9. Reverb by Material → Max zones`, `Only largest box per room`, `Min. absorption`.

### Known issues

- Mostly tested on Factory by one tester; large outdoor maps barely checked with this version (Customs: one raid, checked in the logs).
- Loudness of distant shots with the close recording is still being tuned (the loudness-curve distance works in the logs, but is not judged by ear on the large maps). Switch `Near recording at every distance` off to get SEB's recordings back.
- Distant shots fade out at the game's range limit (indoors ~160–200 m, suppressed outdoors ~240–300 m).
- Shots from a neighbouring hall can sound almost unmuffled; the event limit does not apply to gunshots and impacts yet.
- Keycard reader beeps can still slip out for one audio block.
- Lamp level is an estimate (not yet measured whether the game plays lamps too loud).
- An obstacle in the same hall can muffle more than in reality (the hall's own reverberant field is not added back yet).
- No precomputed path data in this release: obstacles inside buildings without a way around use the screening limit and are heard from the source; no level gain in corridors with a clear view.
- Hollow barrels and some large metal plates count as solid steel.
- The filter part took about 3 % of the main thread on a busy Customs raid, single frames up to 16 ms; first raid on a map measures rooms in the background; reverb zone setup up to about 1 s at raid start on big maps.
- With the close recording switched off, section 13's far-shot curve applies again and can make a shot at 100 m louder than one at 50 m.
- Not tested with Guns Are Loud.

- **New (experimental, off by default): own sound path search, section `19. Path search (experimental)`.** The game only knows the straight line (transmission/diffraction/reflection rays) and its portal routes between rooms; real sound goes around corners through the rooms. The path search (developed offline as "Wegsuche" stages 1–2) uses a network of diffracting edges and openings of the map: one Dijkstra tree per 1 m cell of the listener, then per source the visible network nodes sorted by total length; per path UTD edge diffraction, corridor gain (early image sources), closed door leaves by the mass law (`TuerKern`), the line through the walls with measured thickness.
  - *Data:* a path network file per map, `BepInEx/cache/TrueAcoustics/<map>-<checksum>.wegenetz` (Factory 395 MB file, ~440 MB RAM: 125 099 nodes, 5.0 M sound triangles with their own BVH, 348 openings). It is built on this PC from the game files and never shipped; the checksum covers the reader version and name/size/time of every game file read (36 files on Factory) and is checked at load – after a game update the file is ignored. For now the file comes from the offline tool (`lauf.sh factory export3`, `bereitstellen.sh`); building it in the plugin follows later with the same format.
  - *Runtime:* loaded in the background at raid start; all searching happens on one background thread at 10 Hz (listener tree rebuilt when you leave your 1 m cell or a door changes, 0.3–0.5 s on Factory; meanwhile the last results stay). Per source at most `Max. ms per source` (2 ms) after the first path. The game thread only passes positions and door states (portals matched by scene + portal ID, state live) and picks up finished results. Unity physics is not used: its queries only run on the main thread, so the search rays its own BVH of the same colliders.
  - *Modes:* `CompareOnly` (default) changes no sound and writes a `[PathSearch]` line per logged sound into the sound log (paths with length, dB per band 250 Hz/1 kHz/4 kHz, arrival direction, closed doors, corridor gain; the line through the walls; the sum; next to it the mod's path, T/D/R scores, room path / fallback path and the difference). A MARK also lists the paths of the sources within 40 m. `Use` puts the found paths into the room-path mixer instead of the game's routes (room paths and the fallback path; sounds without a result yet keep the mod's value). Diagnostics every 30 s: search times, budget hits, listener trees, doors, memory.
  - Maps: Factory, Labs (302 MB file, ~350 MB RAM), Labyrinth (84 MB, ~90 MB); the large maps (tiles) follow after the Factory test.
  - Offline test (`Materialklang-WegsucheTest`): the plugin's search gives exactly the stage-2 results on all 12 Factory test pairs, with the scene door states and with all doors closed (24/24, length and level differences 0); background thread with a second thread hammering the interface gives the same results; door switched live → same value as the reference. Labs 16/16 and Labyrinth 16/16 equal as well. Not yet heard in a raid.

- **Path search (section 19): faster under the game's Mono (report `Wegsuche/BERICHT-MONO-LEISTUNG.md`, "way A").** Same results bit for bit (offline test: 3 315 replayed live lines + 32 test pairs identical), measured with Tarkov's own Mono 6.13 under Wine: listener tree 858 → 60 ms, a source 5.2 → 4.0 ms without budget (median 4.7 → 2.3 ms) and 3.3 → 1.5 ms with the 2 ms budget, a new listener position with 16 sources 937 → 104 ms.
  - Edges as flat arrays with their length and door penalty precomputed (no square roots and no door test per edge in the tree); a door that opens or closes resets only its own edges (0.07 ms instead of recalculating all 414 018 portal edges, ~0.6 s).
  - The tree is built to 40 m first and extended on demand – exact: candidates up to the shortest candidate distance + finished radius are complete and in order, so the search only grows the tree when it needs farther nodes.

### Code review fixes

- **Fix: a flashbang 24 m away in the same outdoor area was silent (−60 dB, Ground Zero MARK 1 14:12).** Sound log: `T=1.00 … hits: none (free rays 0/98)`. *Cause:* the calculation did run – all 98 rays had **no passage** (only one side hit something, e.g. the source sits inside a collider or a one-sided mesh, or the two sides hit two different objects). The game counts those as `NoPath`, so T = 1, and in physics mode T = 1 is the full insulation of 60 dB. That is no measurement. *Fix:* when not a single ray gives a result (no free ray, no measured wall; "too thick" still counts as measured), a transmission-only sound (one-shots of `MultiSourceSpatialAudioOccluder`) keeps the **game's own volume and filter** instead of 60 dB. Combined occluders are unchanged (the game takes diffraction/reflection there). Sound log: `(free rays 0/98, no passage: 60 one-sided hit, 38 two objects) no valid ray result …`; BepInEx diagnostics count these cases.
- **Fix: edge diffraction around a 90° corner was up to 24 dB too loud at large deflections (section 14).** The UTD formula only holds up to a deflection of (n − 1)·180° (n = 1.5: 90°); beyond that the listener would be *inside* the wedge and |D| grows again (179°: −41.6 dB instead of −65.7 dB). Now the effective wedge is never wider than the geometry allows: n = max(setting, 1 + deflection/180°) – at 180° the thin edge (n = 2) like Steam Audio. Below 90° nothing changes.
- **Fix: room paths from a source inside a room were too quiet (physics mode).** Only the direct part W·Ω/4π went through the first opening; the reverberant field of the source room was missing.   - *Which sources:* every source whose range True Acoustics extended and that has no occlusion in the game: ambient players (dripping water, campfires, neon signs, factory machines – section 15), raw scene sources (fires on Shoreline/Streets/Ground Zero, Streets generator, idling vans, extract flares), vehicles (V-Ex taxi, BTR engine outdoors), and the scripted sources without occlusion (trains, airdrop plane, BTR turret and air horn, mortar whistle, extract gates – section 18).   - *How:* True Acoustics measures itself – no game occluder is attached (that path was not safe: the taxi drives off and its object is switched off, and `SourceOccluder.LateUpdate` never pauses then). 5 lines from the source to your ears (centre + 4 offset by 0.25 m), two rays each, on worker threads; the hits are combined into wall layers and get the mass law per band (250 Hz / 1 kHz / 4 kHz) with receiving room and flanking – exactly the calculation of `Walls between rooms`. ## 0.8.4-beta

- **New section 14. Sound Paths (physics) – all sound paths in real decibels and added as energy (stage 1 of the "True Acoustics" plan).** Built and checked in the offline test only, not heard in a raid yet. Every part has its own switch (off = the behaviour before it), all on by default in Physics Mode.
  - *Why:* in Physics Mode only the path through the wall (T) was a dB value; the paths around an edge (D) and over a reflection (R) were the game's 0..1 numbers (path length / range of the sound), and the plugin took the minimum of the three. Two problems: an edge 2 m to the side of a bot 8 m away counted as 9 dB no matter how deep the shadow, and wherever the quietest path changed, the level jumped (offline border test: up to 9.5 dB per 0.1 m of movement at a house corner).
  - *Edge diffraction (D):* Edge diffraction uses the UTD implementation from Valve's Steam Audio (Apache 2.0) – `DeviationModel::utdDeviation` ported to C# with the same mathematics (`src/Utd.cs`, notice in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`). Inputs: the edge point the game already finds, the deviation angle source–edge–listener and the distance parameter L = s·s'/(s+s') from the real geometry (Steam Audio uses a fixed 0.05 m). The game has no face data, so the wedge is assumed: `Edge diffraction: wedge angle (deg)` (90 = house corner; 0 = thin edge as in Steam Audio). Checked against the literature in the offline test: deep shadow equals Keller's GTD within 0.05 dB, the shadow boundary at oblique incidence is −5.3 dB (Kouyoumjian–Pathak: −6 dB), grazing incidence (Steam Audio's convention) is 0 dB at the boundary, so the level runs continuously from line of sight into the shadow. Per band 250 Hz / 1 kHz / 4 kHz, so a corner also muffles highs (−6 / −12 / −18 dB at 0.5 rad for L 2.5 m).
  - *Reflection (R):* image source over the shortest reflection path the game finds, `−20 lg(L_R/d) + Σ 10 lg(1 − α(f))` with the absorption of the surfaces actually hit (their collider ids are now recorded per ray during the game's reflection steps; material table of section 9), plus `Reflection: extra loss per bounce (dB)` (3, assumption – the game's last leg to the listener is a visibility test from the hit point, not a mirror path). Replaces the game's 0.7 per bounce and its linear distance loss. Monte-Carlo flicker: a reflection that is not found once is held, after that it fades by 3 dB per calculation; changes under 1 dB are ignored.
  - *Bands and low-pass:* wall insulation is now calculated at 250 Hz, 1 kHz and 4 kHz (mass law + coincidence at each frequency) and the low-pass is fitted to the resulting curve (2-pole fit over 1 kHz and 4 kHz) instead of the free mapping "22 kHz → 800 Hz over the score". Example: 2 cm wood 21 / 33 / 45 dB → low-pass 770 Hz (before 6.9 kHz); 15 cm concrete (60 dB cap) → 950 Hz (before 2.1 kHz). Physics Mode therefore sounds clearly more muffled behind thin walls; `Band low-pass strength` (1.0) scales it. High-pass unchanged (open-path value since 0.8.4).
  - *Energy sum:* T, D and R are added per band as energy (never above 0 dB). Free transmission rays and the edge path are the same sound at an obstacle's edge, so the larger of the two counts. The room route (doors/windows) and the wall between rooms are summed too. Paths the game did not calculate this time (low priority) keep their last value, faded out over 1–3 m of movement. Offline border test (source walks 18 m from line of sight deep into the shadow of a house corner, 66 rays as in the game): largest step 0.2 dB per 0.1 m (before 8.6–9.5 dB), never louder deeper in the shadow. When the game's direct test calls a path "clear" (≤ 1.2 dB), its small value is now kept instead of 0 dB, so there is no step at that threshold either.
  - *Game switches:* the game only looks for a reflection when T > 0.55 **and** D > 0.5 – with the energy sum it would then suddenly add up. `Reflections for every occluded sound` (on) lowers both thresholds for sounds up to `… max. distance (m)` (50). Cost: up to 1 + 2 × 24 casts per reflection step (the game schedules the whole buffer), about as many as T and D together for an occluded close sound; the new diagnostic line counts transmission / diffraction / reflection casts and the extra share every 30 s.
  - *Receiving room:* sound through a wall fills the room you are in (EN ISO 12354-1: P = I·S·τ, `4P/A` diffuse plus direct radiation of the wall into the half-space), with the room box, surface and absorption measured by section 9 (needs section 9 on). `Receiving room: diffuse share` (0.5 – Meta XR may already add reverb in your room), limited to ±6 dB, wall area ≤ 20 m². Outdoors next to a measured building only the direct radiation counts. Example: wall in the middle of 10 m, S = A = 10 m² → +12 dB uncapped (ISO), +6 dB with the default cap.
  - *Flanking:* where your room is known, sound also passes over floor, ceiling and side walls (EN ISO 12354-1 4.4, rigid cross junction K13 = 8.7 + 17.1M + 5.7M², K12 = 8.7 + 5.7M²): 15 cm concrete everywhere 42.6 → 40.8 dB, a light partition in a concrete building almost unchanged. A continuous (non-rigid) timber floor over a wall is not modelled.
  - *Meta XR check (read only):* at raid start the log states Meta XR's acoustic model and whether its own diffraction is on (`[Diagnostics] Meta XR acoustics …`, also in the sound log). An older Player.log (SPT 4.0.13) shows `AcousticRayTracing, diffraction = True`.
  - *Sound log:* each sound line now shows every path in dB at 250 Hz / 1 kHz / 4 kHz (T with its share of free rays and the room/flank correction, D with path length and detour, R with length, bounces and materials, held/kept/extra) and the sum; room-path lines show route, wall (with room/flank) and their sum. **Low-pass jumping by an octave every few frames (bots behind walls)**

- *Cause 1:* if one of the two rays of a measuring line had 8 hits, the whole line counted as blocked – 201 of 230 lines in the Sandbox log, "wall won 0x". Now thin groups get a typical thickness (metal sheet 2 mm, glass 6 mm, posters/plastic 5 mm, thin wood 12 mm, thick wood/doors 40 mm, thick metal 10 mm); solid groups (concrete, soil, …) stay at `Shell thickness`, because there the surface is one side of a slab – the road above the Sandbox car park is real concrete between you and the bot and keeps counting.

**Cause** (found in a Streets sound log of 0.8.0)

## 0.6.0-beta

Developer/tuning tools and three physics fixes. The plugin reports the numeric version 0.6.0.

**New: sound log and live tuning** (F12 section `11. Sound log & live tuning`, both off by default; see the README section "Sound log and live tuning")
- **Sound log:** one line per processed sound within a radius (default 60 m) in `BepInEx/Materialklang-Logs/<date_time>-<map>.log`, one file per raid, last 10 kept, 50 MB cap. Each line: real time and game time, kind/clip/source/owner, distance, path (direct / through walls / diffraction / reflection / via doors / through wall between rooms) with the three scores, the obstacles hit (material group, thickness, dB per piece, collider name), total volume and low-pass, Physics Mode or sliders, timbre and air values, SEB directivity and air values, rooms and reverb zone. Rate-limited per source (default 4 lines/s). Written by a background thread; the game thread only fills a recycled record (no allocation per sound; checked offline). Off = one boolean check.
- **Mark odd sound** key (unbound by default): writes `=== MARK n ===` with time and position into the log.
- **Live tuning:** `BepInEx/config/kaan.materialklang.live.json` with overrides `"Section/Key": value` (exact F12 names) is checked once per second; changed values are written into the settings (and the cfg) without a restart, each one logged (`[Live] key: old -> new`, also in the sound log). `"reload": true` applies everything again. Helper script `materialklang-live.sh`.
- Almost all settings are now read live, including some that used to need the next raid: `Clear-path threshold` (both), door/window values, `Only largest box per room`, Timbre `Enabled`. Every change from F12 or from the file also rebuilds the cached door/wall values and makes the game recalculate all sound occlusion. Still restart-only: `3. Bot Hearing (SAIN) / Mode` and the four `10. Advanced (rays)` settings.

**Fixes**
- **Air absorption slider renamed**: `Humidity` is now `Air clarity factor` with a correct description. The old text said "below 1 = duller (damp air/fog)", which is the wrong way round: by ISO 9613-1 dry air absorbs highs more (8 kHz at 20 °C: ~217 dB/km at 20 % humidity, ~59 dB/km at 100 %). The factor itself works exactly as before and your value is carried over automatically.
- **Snow is its own material group** (`2. 15 Snow`, `Density 15 Snow`, `Solid up to 15 Snow`): before, snow shared group 03 with gravel at 1600 kg/m³ (far too heavy). New defaults 300 kg/m³ and 3 m. Group 03 is now `Gravel, Pebbles` and keeps your values (renamed sections/keys are carried over); snow starts with the slider values of the old shared group, but with the new density. In Physics Mode snow is therefore much quieter to hear through than before (1 m of snow: 51 dB instead of 65 → 60 dB).
- **Coincidence correction for thick heavy walls** (Physics Mode; new settings `Coincidence correction` (on) and `Coincidence loss factor` (0.1)): above its coincidence frequency a thick concrete/stone, thick metal or thick wood piece insulates less than the plain mass law (Sharp's three-region method, README "Coincidence correction"). Typical at 250 Hz: 10 cm concrete −11 dB, 20 cm −8 dB, 1 m about −1 dB. Thin groups (thin wood/metal, glass, door leaf, soil, gravel, snow, soft, body) and any piece with `fc ≥ 2 × reference frequency` are unchanged. The loss factor is a fitted value, not a literature value.

**Other**
- New offline tests `Materialklang-KlangTest` (JSON overrides to cfg keys, rate limiter, line format, writer/rotation/size cap, allocation per entry, acoustics) and extended `Materialklang-MigrationTest` (0.5.0 -> 0.6.0 carry-over).
